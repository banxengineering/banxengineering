## 🏗️ Currently Building

**[TEPA](https://github.com/banxengineering/tepa)** — An open-source desk companion that reacts to your environment. Voice, music, time, and focus — all on your desk. Built on Raspberry Pi with custom PCB design and 3D-printed enclosure.

**Automated Assembly** — Repurposed 3D printers and custom pick-and-place tooling for desktop PCB prototyping cells. Building the factory of one.

**[Techpreneur News](https://techpreneur.beehiiv.com/subscribe)** — Weekly insights at the intersection of engineering, software, and business. Built for makers who also build companies.
